Privacy Policy

AssamRise · Last updated 28 November 2026

Your practice stays on your phone. Every answer you give and everything the app records about your progress is kept on your phone. It is never sent to us, and we cannot see it. The app has no advertising, no analytics and no trackers.

If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your name, your email address and an account number. We use them only to run your account and, if you subscribe, to connect your plan to your account.

Who we are

AssamRise is an exam practice app for Assam government examinations, published by RayCode Studio. You can reach us at contact@assamrise.com.

Signing in with Google

Signing in is optional for practice. You can use the free questions without an account. You need to sign in before you subscribe, so that your plan belongs to your account and follows you to a new phone.

Sign-in uses Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication, both provided by Google. When you sign in:

  • We receive your name, your email address and an account number (a Firebase user ID) that identifies your account.
  • Google, as our sign-in provider, also processes your device's IP address and basic device information (such as the Android version and phone model) to keep sign-in secure and prevent abuse.
  • We do not receive your Google password, your contacts, your Google Drive or anything else from your Google account.

Your name is used to greet you in the app. You can change the name the app greets you by in Profile → Account; that name is stored only on your phone.

What the app stores on your phone

AssamRise saves the following on your device so that your practice continues where you left off. None of it is sent to us.

  • Your settings: chosen exam, app language, question language, text size, reduce motion and vibration.
  • Your practice: the sittings you have started or finished, which days you practised, and your mistakes schedule.
  • Your progress: which questions you answered, how you answered them, your subject and topic records, and the figures the app uses to suggest what to practise next.
  • How many of the free questions you have used, whether your free mock test has been taken, and which questions you opened while subscribed.
  • If you signed in: which account this phone's practice belongs to, and the name the app greets you by.

A phone holds one learner's practice at a time. If you sign out, your practice stays on the phone, locked, until you sign in again with the same Google account.

Subscriptions and payments

When subscriptions are available, payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your card details, UPI details, bank details or billing address.

To confirm your plan, we use RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives your AssamRise account number and the record of your purchase from Google Play — which plan you bought, when, and when it ends — and checks it with Google. It does not receive your practice history or your payment details.

Who receives your data

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising. Only these service providers process it, on our behalf and only to provide the service:

ProviderWhat it receivesWhy
Google (Firebase Authentication, Google Sign-In)Name, email address, account number, IP address and basic device informationSigning you in securely
RevenueCatAccount number and purchase recordsConfirming your subscription

All data sent from the app travels encrypted.

Permissions

  • Internet — used only to sign in and to check your subscription. Practice itself works entirely offline: every question, picture and explanation is already on your phone.
  • Notifications — only if you turn on daily study reminders. Reminders are created on your phone; nothing is sent to us.

AssamRise cannot use your camera, microphone, contacts, files or location.

Android backup

Android includes AssamRise in your phone's own backup to your Google account, unless you have turned backup off in your phone's settings. This is done by Android, not by us, and we cannot read it. It is why your practice history usually comes back when you reinstall the app. After a restore, you sign in with Google again before a signed-in account's practice opens.

How long we keep your data

We keep your account details for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, your sign-in account is deleted straight away, and your subscription records at RevenueCat are deleted within 7 days. Google Play keeps its own record of any purchase under Google's terms.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Profile → Account → Delete account. This deletes your sign-in account and erases this phone's practice history. It cannot be undone, and it does not refund any time left on a plan. You can also ask us by email; see Delete your AssamRise account for both ways and exactly what is deleted.

Children

AssamRise is made for candidates preparing for government recruitment and eligibility examinations. It is not directed at children under 13.

Questions and content

AssamRise practises questions from publicly released past papers of the respective examination bodies. AssamRise is an independent study app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to APSC, SEBA, SLPRB, the Government of Assam or any other examination authority.

Changes to this policy

If what the app collects changes, we will update this page before that version is released, and the change will be visible in the app's Data safety section on Google Play. The date at the top shows when this page last changed.

Contact

Write to contact@assamrise.com with any question about this policy or about your data.
