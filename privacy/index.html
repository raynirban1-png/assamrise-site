Privacy Policy

AssamRise · Last updated 23 November 2026

AssamRise does not collect your data. The app has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no trackers. It does not request internet access, and it makes no network connection of any kind. Everything you do in the app stays on your phone.

Who we are

AssamRise is an exam practice app for Assam government examinations, published by RayCode Studio. You can reach us at contact@assamrise.com.

What the app stores on your phone

AssamRise saves the following on your device so that your practice continues where you left off. None of it is sent to us, and we cannot see any of it.

  • Your settings: chosen exam, app language, question language, text size, reduce motion and vibration.
  • Your practice: the sittings you have started or finished, which days you practised, and your mistakes schedule.
  • Your progress: which questions you answered, how you answered them, your subject and topic records, and the figures the app uses to suggest what to practise next.
  • How many of the free questions you have used.

Uninstalling the app removes all of it from your phone.

Android backup

Android includes AssamRise in your phone's own backup to your Google account, unless you have turned backup off in your phone's settings. This is done by Android, not by us. The backup goes to your own Google account under Google's terms, and we have no access to it and cannot read it.

This is why your practice history and your free-question count usually come back when you reinstall the app on the same Google account. If you turn Android backup off, they will not.

Permissions

AssamRise requests no permissions. It cannot use your camera, microphone, contacts, files, location or network.

Payments

If you subscribe, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your card details, your bank details or your billing address. Google Play records your purchase against your Google account; that is what restores your subscription if you reinstall the app or change phone. Google's own privacy policy governs that transaction.

Children

AssamRise is made for candidates preparing for government recruitment and eligibility examinations. It is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no data from anyone.

Questions and content

AssamRise practises questions from publicly released past papers of the respective examination bodies. AssamRise is an independent study app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to APSC, SEBA, SLPRB, the Government of Assam or any other examination authority.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts collecting data — for example if we add sign-in or progress sync across devices — we will update this page before that version is released, and the change will be visible in the app's Data safety section on Google Play.

Contact

Write to contact@assamrise.com with any question about this policy or about what the app stores.
