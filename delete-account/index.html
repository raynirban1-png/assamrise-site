Delete your AssamRise account

AssamRise — PYQ Practice · published by RayCode Studio · Last updated 28 November 2026

You can delete your AssamRise account and its data at any time, in the app or by email.

In the app (quickest)

  1. Open AssamRise and go to Profile.
  2. Open Account and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm with your Google account when asked.

By email

If you no longer have the app, write to contact@assamrise.com from the email address of the Google account you signed in with, with the subject “Delete my AssamRise account”. We reply to confirm, and complete the deletion within 7 days.

What is deleted

DataWhat happens
Your sign-in account (name, email address, account number)Deleted straight away when you delete in the app; within 7 days when you ask by email
Your subscription records at RevenueCatDeleted within 7 days
Practice history on your phoneErased when you delete in the app. If you ask by email, we cannot reach your phone: uninstall the app to remove it.

What is kept

  • Google Play's record of a purchase is kept by Google under Google's terms, as for any Play purchase. We cannot delete it.
  • Your phone's Android backup in your own Google account may still contain an older copy of the app's data until Android replaces it. You can delete it from your Google account's backups.
  • We keep no other copy of your data.

Before you delete

Deleting cannot be undone. It does not refund any time left on a plan. If you sign in again later with the same Google account, you start with a new, empty account.
