What it does

Practise the way that suits you

Sit a whole past paper as it was set, work through one topic at a time, answer ten mixed questions when you have a few minutes, or take a timed full mock test.

Every answer explained

Why the right answer is right, why the other options are wrong, the rule or formula behind it, the faster way to reach it, and what to watch for next time.

Your mistakes come back

Wrong answers are kept and returned to you on a schedule until you get them right twice — and the app shows you the pattern in them, not just a list.

Practice that measures itself

Your accuracy by subject and by topic, which subjects are costing you marks, and what to practise next — worked out from your own answers.

Assamese and English

The whole app reads in either language, and you can switch at any time. Questions appear in the language they were printed in.

Works without internet

Every question, picture and explanation is already on your phone. Practise on the bus, in a village with no signal, or with data switched off — it makes no difference.

Nothing is collected

No advertising, no tracking, no analytics. Your practice history stays on your phone and never reaches us.
